Centro Sur

Centro Sur is an artist residency based in Buenos Aires, dedicated to promoting international and intercultural exchange through an active dialogue with the local artistic landscape. Its aim is to host artists and theorists from diverse backgrounds, offering a proactive and participatory environment that fosters an ongoing exchange between local and international contexts.

Centro Sur

The purpose of Centro Sur lies in the desire to bring the presence and research of international contemporary art to one of the major capitals of Latin America, exploring territories of syncretism and interculturality. In continuous dialogue with the city of Buenos Aires and its inhabitants, the project investigates the possibility of creating an entity that encourages sustained interaction between local and international artistic production, transforming the artistic experience into a collaborative process.

About

Centro Sur works in synergy with the city's main cultural institutions, connecting the activities of each organization so that they converge toward a shared objective: positioning Buenos Aires as a hub for experimentation, research, and production in contemporary art.
